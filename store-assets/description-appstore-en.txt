Every night, one NBA game is drawn at random. Read it better than everyone else.

NBgAmes is a free NBA prediction game. No ads, no real money.
One game a day, three questions, one leaderboard. Nothing more, nothing less.


TONIGHT'S PREDICTION

Each night the app draws one game from the NBA slate, along with a random stat:
points, assists, rebounds, blocks or steals. You have until one hour before
tip-off to answer.

- Which team wins? +10 points
- Who will lead that stat? +20 points
- Exactly how many will they get? +30 points

All three right? Your total is doubled. 120 points on the table every night.

Results are pulled automatically after the game and points credited without you
having to do a thing.


THE MINI-GAMES

NBA Trivia: a daily 10-question quiz drawn from over 650: champions, MVPs,
drafts, nicknames, trades, arenas, records, nationalities. Three difficulty
modes, up to 100 points a day.

Summer Trades: across 50 players, guess who stays, who leaves, and for which
franchise. Results land when the trade window closes.


THE LEADERBOARDS

Global, last game, week, month. Plus archives of completed seasons, so past
standings never disappear.

A streak tracks your consistency: it grows with every prediction you place and
resets to zero the moment you skip a game.


ALSO IN THE APP

- Theme in your favourite franchise's colours
- Light and dark mode
- French and English
- Detailed history of all your predictions
- Community trends on every game
