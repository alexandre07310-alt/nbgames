2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
NBgAmes is a free NBA prediction game. Each night the app randomly selects one
NBA game plus one random statistic (points, assists, rebounds, blocks, steals).
Users answer three questions before tip-off: winning team (10 pts), player who
will lead that stat (20 pts), exact number (30 pts). All three correct doubles
the score. Results are fetched automatically after the game.
Also included: a daily NBA trivia quiz (650+ questions) and a summer transfer
prediction game.
Audience: NBA fans aged 13+, primarily French-speaking.
NOT a gambling app: no wagers, no real-money prizes, no in-app purchases, no
advertising. Points have no monetary value.
