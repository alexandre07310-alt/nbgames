2. APP PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

NBgAmes is a free NBA prediction game for basketball fans.

Problem it solves: casual NBA fans want a light, daily way to engage with the
league and compete with friends, without the complexity of fantasy leagues or
the risks of gambling apps.

How it works: every night the app automatically selects one NBA game at random,
together with one randomly chosen statistic (points, assists, rebounds, blocks
or steals). Users answer three questions before tip-off: which team wins
(10 points), which player will lead that statistic (20 points), and the exact
number they will record (30 points). All three correct doubles the score.
Results are fetched automatically after the game and points are credited.

Two secondary mini-games are included: a daily NBA trivia quiz (10 questions
drawn from a database of 650+), and a summer transfer prediction game.

Target audience: NBA fans aged 13 and over, primarily French-speaking.

IMPORTANT: NBgAmes is NOT a gambling app. There are no wagers, no real-money
prizes, no in-app purchases and no advertising. Points have no monetary value
and exist solely for the leaderboard.

Steps to review the main features:

1. Launch the app and sign in with the demo credentials above
   (or tap "Inscription" to create a new account, or use Sign in with Apple).
2. MATCH tab — the game of the night, the countdown until predictions close,
   and the three prediction questions. Predictions close one hour before
   tip-off; if predictions are closed at review time, the previous night's
   result is shown instead.
3. MINI-JEUX tab — "Culture NBA" is playable at any time: tap any answer to
   see scoring and haptic feedback.
4. CLASSEMENT tab — global, last game, week, month, and archived seasons.
5. PROFIL tab — edit profile, share, and ACCOUNT DELETION at the bottom
   of the screen ("Supprimer mon compte"). Deletion requires typing the word
   SUPPRIMER to confirm, then permanently removes the account, the profile
   photo, all predictions and all scores.

Note on timing: the daily game is drawn from the real NBA schedule. Outside the
NBA season, or between games, the MATCH tab shows a countdown rather than an
open prediction form. The mini-games remain fully playable at all times.


──────────────────────────────────────────────
4. EXTERNAL SERVICES USED
──────────────────────────────────────────────

- Firebase Authentication (Google LLC) — account creation and sign-in
  (email/password and Sign in with Apple).
- Cloud Firestore (Google LLC) — storage of user profiles, predictions,
  scores and leaderboards.
- Firebase Storage (Google LLC) — optional profile photos.
- Firebase Hosting (Google LLC) — hosting of the privacy policy, support
  and account-deletion pages.
- ESPN public sports API — NBA schedules, live scores and box-score
  statistics. Read-only. No user data is ever sent to this service.

No advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no payment processor, no AI service.


──────────────────────────────────────────────
5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The app functions identically in all regions. There are no region-locked
features, no region-specific content and no geographic restrictions.

The interface is available in French and English; the language follows the
user's preference and can be changed in the app.

All users worldwide see the same NBA games, the same questions and the same
global leaderboard.

──────────────────────────────────────────────

NBgAmes is an independent fan application. It is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or sponsored by the NBA or any of its franchises. This is
stated explicitly in the App Store description, in the app's support page

The app displays factual sports information only — game schedules, final

Team names and team logos are referenced for identification purposes only,
to indicate which teams are playing. They are not used as branding for the

presented in a way that suggests any official association.

The app is free, carries no advertising and generates no revenue.

NBgAmes does not operate in a regulated industry. It is not a gambling
application: there are no stakes, no wagers, no real-money prizes and no
in-app purchases. Points have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged
